How it works

20 of 80 numbers are drawn. One of the 20 is the Bulls-eye. Bet is $2 base, plus $2 Bulls-eye if ON ($4 total). If Bulls-eye is ON and one of your matched numbers is the Bulls-eye, you win the Bulls-eye prize (it replaces the base prize). Otherwise you win the base prize.
